Research

My work asks how untrusting parties can share a ledger and hold each other accountable: making BFT consensus faster (with TEEs) and answerable (accountability proofs), scaling payments off-chain (PCN models and pooling), and proving the integrity and availability of sensitive data. On the application side I study authentication, authorization, audit and credit scoring for financial systems, and more recently how LLM agents can transact and be audited on such infrastructure.
