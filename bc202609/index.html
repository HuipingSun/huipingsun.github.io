从密码学与去中心化网络出发，理解比特币、以太坊、DeFi 与稳定币的技术逻辑和商业含义。每讲附设 30 分钟「AI 研究方法」模块，学习用大模型做文献调研、数据分析与报告写作，并直接用于本课程作业与项目。 Starting from cryptography and decentralized networks, this course explains the technical logic and business implications of Bitcoin, Ethereum, DeFi and stablecoins. Each lecture includes a 30-minute "AI for Research" module on using large language models for literature review, data analysis and report writing, applied directly to the course assignments and project.

课程简介About

面向工程管理硕士，兼顾技术原理与管理决策视角。三条线索并行：技术基础（哈希、共识、智能合约）、应用与治理（加密货币、稳定币、DeFi、产业与政务应用、监管合规）、AI 研究方法（提示工程、文献调研、数据分析、AI 辅助写作）。先修要求：无，具备基本编程概念即可。 Designed for Master of Engineering Management students, balancing technical principles with a management and decision-making perspective. Three threads run in parallel: technical foundations (hashing, consensus, smart contracts); applications and governance (cryptocurrencies, stablecoins, DeFi, industry and government use, regulation); and AI for research (prompt engineering, literature search, data analysis, AI-assisted writing). No prerequisites beyond basic programming concepts.
